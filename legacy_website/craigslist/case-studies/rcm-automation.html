RCM Automation — Medical Billing Company

Healthcare Automation Billing Efficiency

Automated medical billing workflows, reducing staff from 700 to 20

"We went from hundreds of manual billing tasks to an automated RCM workflow. Our team size dropped while throughput improved dramatically."

— Medical Billing Director

The Problem

Large billing operation was heavily manual — high labor costs, delayed claims processing, and inefficient workflows. Key challenges included:

  • 700+ staff handling manual billing tasks
  • High error rates in claim processing
  • Delayed cash flow due to manual workflows
  • Difficulty scaling operations
  • Compliance risks with manual processes

The Solution

Implemented automated RCM workflows including claim status checks, denial management triage, auto-appeal drafts, and task orchestration. Integrated with existing EHR/EMR and clearinghouse connectors.

Automated Workflows

Intelligent automation of claim submission, status tracking, and denial management processes

Performance Analytics

Real-time dashboards showing claim status, revenue metrics, and workflow efficiency

The Outcome

Company moved from manual-heavy operations to automated workflows and was able to serve the same workload with ~20 staff instead of ~700, improving margins and turnaround time.
