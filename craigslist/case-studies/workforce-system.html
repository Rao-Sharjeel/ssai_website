Workforce Supervision & Productivity System — Tech Company HR

Productivity HR Analytics Remote Work

A reliable productivity and attendance reporting system — we can reward productive workers accurately

"We finally have trustable metrics for performance. The system helps us make better decisions about rewards and capacity planning."

— HR Director

The Problem

Company needed insight into workforce productivity across remote and on-prem users, facing challenges with:

  • Lack of visibility into remote worker productivity
  • Difficulty measuring performance objectively
  • Inconsistent reward distribution
  • Poor capacity planning

The Solution

Built an employee activity monitoring & reporting system that captures usage metrics, productivity signals, and generates per-worker time/output reports. Integrates with payroll/performance review workflows.

Productivity Analytics

Real-time monitoring of work patterns and output metrics

Performance Reports

Individual and team performance dashboards

The Outcome

Improved transparency, better reward decisions for high-performers, and more accurate capacity planning.
