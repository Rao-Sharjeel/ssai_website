ERP Enhancement — Small-Biz ERP with AI

ERP AI Automation Manufacturing

AI features extended the life of our ERP and reduced manual errors during invoicing and procurement

"AI kept our ERP relevant and smarter. The forecasting and anomaly detection features have transformed our decision-making."

— Operations Manager

The Problem

Legacy ERP lacked intelligence for forecasting and anomaly detection, making manual reconciliation costly and error-prone:

  • No demand forecasting capabilities
  • Manual anomaly detection in spending
  • Inefficient procurement processes
  • High error rates in invoicing
  • Difficulty scaling with business growth

The Solution

Implemented AI modules for forecasting demand, anomaly detection in spends, and smart procurement suggestions that plug into the existing ERP system.

Demand Forecasting

AI-powered demand prediction using historical data and market trends

Anomaly Detection

Automated detection of unusual spending patterns and potential errors

The Outcome

Reduced errors and improved decision-making, allowing the ERP to remain the company's central system while adding modern AI capabilities.
