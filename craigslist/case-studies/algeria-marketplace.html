Tourism & Artisan Marketplace — Algeria

Marketplace Tourism Mobile Ecommerce

We launched a marketplace for artisans and connected them with tourists — idea to app to market-ready

"The app turned ideas into customers. We connected local artisans with international tourists seamlessly."

— Marketplace Founder

The Problem

Artisans needed a platform to sell to tourists and export unique crafts, facing challenges with:

  • No digital presence for local artisans
  • Difficulty reaching international tourists
  • Lack of payment processing
  • No shipping coordination

The Solution

Built a mobile-first app and admin panel for listings, payments, and shipping integration. Launched a marketing campaign focused on tourism channels and marketplaces.

Mobile App

Native mobile app for tourists to browse and purchase artisan goods

Admin Panel

Comprehensive dashboard for artisans to manage listings and orders

The Outcome

Launched MVP to market with initial merchant onboardings and traction from tourist channels.
