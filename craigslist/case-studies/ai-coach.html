AI Coach Assistant — Business Coach

AI Coaching Automation Service

An AI assistant that speaks the coach's voice — more client touchpoints without adding hours

"The AI assistant is like having a junior coach on staff. It handles routine questions and frees me up for high-value client work."

— Business Coach

The Problem

Coach had limited time for client follow-ups and basic Q&A, leading to:

  • Delayed responses to client questions
  • Missed opportunities for engagement
  • Time spent on repetitive questions
  • Difficulty scaling client support

The Solution

Built and trained a bespoke AI chatbot to emulate the coach's style and answer common client questions, triage requests, and hand off to human when needed. Deployed on website + WhatsApp.

Voice Training

AI trained on coach's communication style, tone, and common responses

Multi-Platform

Deployed on website and WhatsApp for maximum client accessibility

The Outcome

Coach regained hours each week, scaled client support without hiring staff, and preserved brand voice.
